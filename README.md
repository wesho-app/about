## We + Shop

### Fast Commerce for the Next Billion Consumers

**Wesho** is building a fast and convenient local-commerce platform for **villages, small towns, and emerging markets in India**—starting from **Madhya Pradesh**.

While India's quick-commerce revolution has transformed how people shop in major cities, a massive part of the country remains underserved.

**Wesho is built for that market.**

---

# The Opportunity

India's digital commerce story is no longer limited to metropolitan cities.

Smartphones, affordable internet, UPI, digital payments, and increasing consumer expectations are reaching smaller towns and villages rapidly.

But the commerce experience has not evolved at the same speed.

In many smaller communities, customers still depend heavily on:

* Physically visiting local stores
* Calling shopkeepers to ask about products
* Uncertainty about product availability
* Limited product discovery
* Cash-based transactions
* No convenient home delivery
* Fragmented local shopping

At the same time, local retailers already have something extremely valuable:

> **Inventory + customers + local trust + physical presence.**

What they often lack is the technology and logistics layer that connects all of those assets digitally.

### This is where Wesho comes in.

---

# Technology

Wesho is being built as a modern digital commerce platform.

The technology stack includes:

### Mobile

**Flutter + Dart**

A cross-platform architecture allows the product to evolve efficiently across supported platforms.

### State Management

**Riverpod**

For scalable and predictable application state.

### Networking

**Dio + Retrofit**

For structured API communication.

### Navigation

**AutoRoute**

For scalable navigation and routing.

### Backend

API-driven architecture designed for authentication, products, orders, payments, and delivery.

### Database

**MySQL**

For structured transactional commerce data.

### Payments

**Razorpay + Cash on Delivery**

### Notifications

**Firebase Cloud Messaging**

The technology is designed to support the product's current requirements while leaving room for future scale.
